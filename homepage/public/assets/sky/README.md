# Celestial assets

- `moon-lroc.jpg`: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (2025 color
  map), based on Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter / LROC data. Credit: NASA's
  Scientific Visualization Studio; Ernie Wright; NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University.
  Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
  Original: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg
- Moon illumination: a TypeScript subset adapted from SunCalc 1.9.0 by Vladimir
  Agafonkin, under the BSD-2-Clause license retained in `SUNCALC-LICENSE.txt`.
  Source: https://github.com/mourner/suncalc/tree/v1.9.0

The moon updates its approximate geocentric phase hourly and when the page becomes
visible. The view stays north-up; local horizon orientation and libration are not
modeled. The sun and the orbit are decorative, not an observer's sky position.
